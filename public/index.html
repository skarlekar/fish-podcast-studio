Step 1

The brief

Two prompts drive Claude. The system prompt sets the voice and craft; the user prompt is the story. Edit either one. Nothing is voiced until you approve the script.

Turn a story into a two-host podcast: a male and a female host debate and banter their way through it, then leave the listener with takeaways and a question. The system prompt sets the show; the user prompt is the story. Nothing is voiced until you approve the script.

{{male}} and {{female}} are replaced by the host names.

Always added after your system prompt: length, audio tags, output format
